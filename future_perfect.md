# Future Perfect

## When to use it

1. **An action that will be completed before a specific time in the future**.
   - I will have finished my work by Friday.
   - By 2025, she will have graduated.

2. **To show that one future action will be completed before another**.
   - By the time you arrive, I will have cooked dinner.

## Form

- **Affirmative**: will + have + past participle.
  - I/you/he/she/it/we/they + will + have + V3.

- **Negative**: won't + have + past participle.
  - I won't have finished.

- **Question**: Will + subject + have + past participle?
  - Will you have arrived by then?

## Common time markers

by, by the time, before, in (a year), by then.

## Examples

- I will have finished my work by Friday.
- We will have learned a lot of words by December.
- You will have finished by 5 pm.
- They will have left the hotel by 8 pm.
- They will have arrived at the hotel by 8 pm.
- We will have finished the building by December.

## Watch out

- Do not confuse with the simple future: the future perfect emphasizes completion before a future deadline.
