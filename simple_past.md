# Simple Past

## When to use it

1. **Completed actions in the past** (at a specific time).
   - I visited my grandmother last weekend.
   - I went to Paris in 2020.

2. **Habits or routines in the past**.
   - She walked to school every day when she was a child.
   - We played soccer every Sunday.

3. **Past facts or states**.
   - He was a teacher for 10 years.
   - The Earth was believed to be flat.

4. **Sequential actions in a story**.
   - First she opened the door, then she turned on the light.
   - He woke up, took a shower, and left for work.

## Form

- **Affirmative**: verb in the past tense.
  - Regular verbs: base + ed (play → played, work → worked).
  - Irregular verbs: memorize them (go → went, see → saw, have → had).
  - He worked yesterday. / She went to Paris.

- **Negative**: didn't + base verb.
  - I didn't go to work yesterday.
  - They didn't see the movie.

- **Question**: Did + subject + base verb.
  - Did you watch the movie?
  - Did she call you?

## The verb "to be" in the past

- Singular: was (I/he/she/it was).
- Plural: were (you/we/they were).
- She was happy. / Were they at home?
- Do not use "did" with "to be".

## Common time markers

Yesterday
Last (week, month, year, weekend, night)
Last (any day), e.g. last Monday
On (any day), e.g. on Sunday
The day before yesterday
Earlier today
In the past
During my childhood
Back in 2015
Ago (any time), e.g. two days ago, five years ago, six months ago
In 2020, in July 2020
When I was young, when I was a child
A long time ago, at that time, in those days

## Examples

- I ate pizza for lunch.
- I lived in Malta from 2024 to 2025.
- I went to the university when I was 18.
- I cooked pasta last weekend.
- Last Winter, we traveled to the Balkans.
- I didn't eat last night, because I was on a diet.
- She didn't do anything wrong when it happened.
- Did you study for the exam?
- Did she call her mom last month?
- Didn't she pass the test?
- We finished the homework.
- Did you eat a hot dog last night?
- They visited their grandparents yesterday.
- I prepared the dinner yesterday.
- We received a cart yesterday.
- I forgot my phone.
- My brother opened the window this morning.
- They closed the store early.

## Watch out

- Do not confuse with the present perfect: the simple past is used for finished actions at a specific time in the past.
