# Simple Present

## When to use it

1. **Habits and routines**.
   - I drink coffee every morning.
   - She goes to the gym on Mondays.

2. **General facts and permanent truths**.
   - Water boils at 100 °C.
   - The Earth revolves around the Sun.

3. **States and likes** (verbs like like, know, want, need, believe).
   - She likes pizza.
   - I know the answer.

4. **Fixed schedules and programs** (trains, classes, events).
   - The class starts at 9:00.
   - The train leaves at 8:30.

5. **Instructions and rules**.
   - You turn left at the bank.
   - Press the button to start.

## Form

- **Affirmative**: subject + base verb.
  - I/you/we/they + base verb (work, play, go).
  - He/she/it + verb + s (works, plays, goes).
    - Third person: -s, -es (go → goes), or -ies (study → studies).

- **Negative**: don't / doesn't + base verb.
  - I don't work on Sundays.
  - She doesn't like coffee.

- **Question**: Do / Does + subject + base verb.
  - Do you speak French?
  - Does he play soccer?

## Common time markers

always, usually, often, sometimes, never, every day, on Mondays, once a week.

## Examples

- Do you work here?
- Does he drink orange juice?
- Does she walk with you in the mornings?
- What kind of music does Juan like to listen to?
- Does Andrea study every day?
- Mario, do you work on weekends?
- What do you do?
- What do you do for work?
- What do you do all morning?
- Andrea, do you like BTS?
- Andrea sews pillows.
- What activities do you do?
- What do you listen to when you go to work?
- I prefer to sleep 8 hours.
- How often do you talk with your mom?
- How often do you talk with friends?
- I believe that ramen is the best food in South Korea.
- I'm happy.
- I work every day.
- They eat pizza on Fridays.
- I walk in the park every morning.
- My dad drives a bus.
- My grandmother prepares cookies every Sunday.

## Watch out

- Do not use "do/does" with the verb "to be".
  - She is happy. (not "She does be happy.")
- Stative verbs (know, like, want, need, believe) are not usually used in the continuous form.
