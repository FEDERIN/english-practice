# Presente Continuo (Present Progressive)

## Uso
- Acciones que ocurren en este momento.
- Planes futuros cercanos.

## Estructura
- Afirmativa: Sujeto + am/is/are + verbo-ing.
  - I am wearing red shorts.
  - She is cooking right now.
- Negativa: Sujeto + am/is/are + not + verbo-ing.
  - I'm not wearing a jacket.
- Pregunta: Am/Is/Are + sujeto + verbo-ing?
  - Are you wearing a hat?

## Ejemplos de las notas
- I wear a blue t-shirt. (presente simple)
- I'm wearing red shorts. (presente continuo)
- I have yellow socks and white shoes.
- My mom is in the kitchen right now, but where are my cousins? Are they in the garden?
- What are you doing? (presente continuo: acción en curso)
- Are you studying English every day?
- Are you playing soccer in the park?
- Are they learning English every day?
- Are we practicing each day or not?
- It's starting to become the best food.
- I am training to drive each day.
- I am listening to this class on my phone.
- She is running and training every morning because of her competition.
- I am working every day.
- We are finishing the homework.
- They are playing soccer.
- We are preparing the dinner.
- I am reading a book.
- You are listening to music.
- My sister is cleaning the plates.
- The children are drawing animals.
- They are closing the store early.
- We are listening to the radio.
- I am organizing my bedroom.
- I am studying English.

## Nota
- "wear" (presente simple) vs "am wearing" (presente continuo): se usa "am wearing" para describir lo que se lleva puesto ahora mismo.

## Ortografía del verbo-ing
- Verbo terminado en "e" muda: se quita la "e" y se agrega "-ing".
  - make → making, write → writing, lie → lying (ie → ying)
- Verbo de una sílaba terminado en consonante-vocal-consonante: se duplica la última consonante.
  - run → running, sit → sitting, swim → swimming
- Verbo terminado en consonante + "y" o vocal + consonante: se agrega "-ing" directamente.
  - play → playing, read → reading, drink → drinking
