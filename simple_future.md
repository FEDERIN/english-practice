# Simple Future

## When to use it

1. **Spontaneous decisions** (made at the moment of speaking).
   - It's cold. I will close the window.
   - I'll have the chicken, please.

2. **Promises and offers**.
   - I will help you with your homework.
   - I'll carry that bag for you.

3. **Predictions** (based on opinion or belief, not evidence).
   - I think it will rain tomorrow.
   - She will be a great doctor.

4. **Future facts that do not depend on us**.
   - The sun will rise at 6:00 AM.
   - Water will freeze at 0 °C.

## Form

- **Affirmative**: will + base verb.
  - I will travel next month.
  - She will work tomorrow.

- **Negative**: won't (will not) + base verb.
  - She won't come to the party.
  - I won't forget.

- **Question**: Will + subject + base verb.
  - Will you call me later?
  - Will they arrive on time?

## Common time markers

tomorrow, next week/month/year, soon, in the future, later.

## Examples

- You will travel tomorrow.
- They will buy a new car.
- We will travel to Brazil next month.
- I will buy a bicycle next year.

## Watch out

- For already decided plans or intentions, use **going to**, not will.
  - I am going to visit my parents this weekend. (already decided)
  - I will visit my parents this weekend. (spontaneous decision or promise)
- "will" does not change with the subject (no -s in third person).
