## Reglas consolidadas

### Presente simple
- Afirmativa: sujeto + verbo base. Negativa: sujeto + do/does + not + verbo base. Pregunta: do/does + sujeto + verbo base.
- Tercera persona (he/she/it): -s, -es (go → goes) o -ies (study → studies).

### Simple present
- Affirmative: subject + base verb; third person singular adds -s/-es/-ies. Negative: don't/doesn't + base verb. Question: Do/Does + subject + base verb.
- Used for habits, facts, states, schedules, and instructions.

### Present perfect
- Affirmative: have/has + past participle. Negative: haven't/hasn't + past participle. Question: Have/Has + subject + past participle.
- Used for unspecified past actions, experiences, and actions continuing to the present (for/since).

### Present perfect continuous
- Affirmative: have/has + been + verb-ing. Negative: haven't/hasn't + been + verb-ing. Question: Have/Has + subject + been + verb-ing.
- Used for actions that started in the past and are still continuing, or just finished with a visible result.

### Simple past
- Affirmative: verb in past tense (regular base + ed; irregular memorize). Negative: didn't + base verb. Question: Did + subject + base verb.
- To be in past: was (singular) / were (plural); no "did".

### Past continuous
- Affirmative: was/were + verb-ing. Negative: wasn't/weren't + verb-ing. Question: Was/Were + subject + verb-ing.
- Used for actions in progress in the past, interrupted actions, and simultaneous actions.

### Past perfect
- Affirmative: had + past participle. Negative: hadn't + past participle. Question: Had + subject + past participle.
- Used for actions completed before another past action.

### Past perfect continuous
- Affirmative: had + been + verb-ing. Negative: hadn't + been + verb-ing. Question: Had + subject + been + verb-ing.
- Used for actions in progress before another past event, emphasizing duration.

### Simple future
- Affirmative: will + base verb. Negative: won't + base verb. Question: Will + subject + base verb.
- For already decided plans, use "going to" instead of "will".

### Future continuous
- Affirmative: will + be + verb-ing. Negative: won't + be + verb-ing. Question: Will + subject + be + verb-ing.
- Used for actions in progress at a specific time in the future.

### Future perfect
- Affirmative: will + have + past participle. Negative: won't + have + past participle. Question: Will + subject + have + past participle.
- Used for actions completed before a specific future time.

### Future perfect continuous
- Affirmative: will + have + been + verb-ing. Negative: won't + have + been + verb-ing. Question: Will + subject + have + been + verb-ing.
- Used for actions in progress up to a specific future time, emphasizing duration.

### Presente continuo
- Afirmativa: am/is/are + verbo-ing. Negativa: + not. Pregunta: Am/Is/Are + sujeto + verbo-ing.
- Ortografía -ing: quitar la "e" muda (make → making); duplicar consonante en verbos CVC de una sílaba (run → running); en el resto, solo -ing (play → playing).
- "wear" (simple) vs "am wearing" (lo que se lleva puesto ahora).

### There is / There are
- Singular: There is + a/an + singular. Plural: There are + plural.
- Negativas: isn't / aren't; "not any" en lugar de "no" + sustantivo.
- Preguntas: Is there any…? / Are there any…?
- Some en ofertas y peticiones; any en preguntas y negativas.

### There was / There were (pasado)
- There was con singular o incontable; There were con plural.
- Negativas: There wasn't / There weren't.
- Some/any funcionan igual que en presente.

### How many / How much
- How many con contables en plural; How much con no contables.
- People es contable: how many.

### Modal verbs
- Sujeto + modal + verbo base, sin "to" ni -s en tercera persona (she can eat).
- Negativas: can't / couldn't / shouldn't / must not / may not.
- Preguntas: modal + sujeto + verbo base (Can you drive a car?).

### Adjetivos
- Afirmativa: sujeto + to be + adjetivo (We're hungry) o adjetivo + sustantivo (a beautiful day). Negativa: sujeto + to be + not + adjetivo. Pregunta: to be + sujeto + adjetivo?
- No cambian en plural ni en género.

### Minimal pairs (pronunciation)
- Short, relaxed vowels (/ɪ/, /ʊ/, /ʌ/) vs. long, tense vowels (/iː/, /uː/): ship /ʃɪp/ – sheep /ʃiːp/.
- Open vowels: bed /bɛd/ – bad /bæd/; hat /hæt/ – hot /hɒt/.
- Voiced vs. voiceless: ice /aɪs/ – eyes /aɪz/; bus /bʌs/ – buzz /bʌz/.
- Difficult consonants: think /θɪŋk/ – sink /sɪŋk/; light /laɪt/ – right /raɪt/.
- See minimal_pairs.md for the full list.

### Vocabulario

## Casa / ubicación (notas generales)

- My house is near the school, but my friends' houses aren't near.
- The house is small, but it's beautiful.
- My room is on the second floor, and the kitchen is on the first floor.
- My mom is in the kitchen right now, but where are my cousins? Are they in the garden?
- The garden is beautiful, and the flowers are everywhere.
- Today is Tuesday.
- My dogs are also happy, but they aren't in the garden right now.
- My house is a comfortable and blessed place.

## Números / cantidades

- one elephant
- two computers
- five people
- three children
- ten chairs

### Regla
- Singular: There is + a/an + sustantivo singular.
- Plural: There are + número + sustantivos en plural.
