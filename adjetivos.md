# Adjetivos

## Uso
- Describen sustantivos o estados. Se colocan antes del sustantivo o después del verbo "to be".

## Estructura
- Afirmativa: sujeto + to be + adjetivo.
  - We're hungry.
  - The teacher Tatiana is intelligent.
  - That crow is suspicious.
- Negativa: sujeto + to be + not + adjetivo.
  - We aren't hungry.
  - The teacher Tatiana isn't intelligent.
  - That crow isn't suspicious.
- Pregunta: to be + sujeto + adjetivo?
  - Are we hungry?
  - Is the teacher Tatiana intelligent?
  - Is that crow suspicious?
- Adjetivo + sustantivo.
  - Today is a beautiful day.
  - It's not a beautiful day.
  - Is it a beautiful day?
- Los adjetivos no cambian en plural ni en género.

## Vocabulario
- hungry = hambriento
- clever = listo / inteligente
- beautiful = hermoso
- intelligent = inteligente
- suspicious = sospechoso
- magnificent = magnífico
- flattered = halagado
- shiny = brillante

## Ejemplos de práctica (con corrección)
- We're hungry.
- She's good with maths. She's really clever.
- The cat's teeth are so shiny.
- Today is a beautiful day.
- The teacher Tatiana is intelligent.
- That crow is suspicious.
