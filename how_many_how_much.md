# How many / How much

## Uso
- **How many** → cosas contables (cuántos/as)
- **How much** → cosas no contables (cuánto/a)

## Estructura
- How many + sustantivo contable plural + verbo?
  - How many countries are there in the USA?
- How much + sustantivo no contable + verbo?
  - How much cheese does the pizza have?
  - How much English do you practice?
  - How much juice do you drink per month?
  - How much pasta does she eat per year?
  - How much water is in the Nile River?

## Errores comunes a evitar
- "How much people do in the concert?" → "How many people are at the concert?" (people es contable → how many).

## Medidas y cantidades
- I drink 8 glasses of water a day.
- I drink 7 glasses of water a day.
- I drink 2 glasses of water.
- Can you measure the house?
- I don't know, I don't measure to drink water per day.

## Expresión
- on the tip of my tongue = en la punta de la lengua.
