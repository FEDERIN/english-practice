# Past Perfect

## When to use it

1. **An action completed before another action in the past**.
   - When I arrived, they had already left.
   - You had learned new words before the test.

2. **To show the order of two past events**.
   - She had finished cooking when the guests arrived.

## Form

- **Affirmative**: had + past participle.
  - I/you/he/she/it/we/they + had + V3 (worked, gone, learned).

- **Negative**: hadn't + past participle.
  - I hadn't finished.

- **Question**: Had + subject + past participle?
  - Had you studied?

## Common time markers

before, after, by the time, already, just, when, never, ever.

## Examples

- You had learned new words.
- You had studied after the class.
- You had studied before the class.
- We had finished the project.
- I had lost my phone.
- We had saved the documents.

## Watch out

- Do not confuse with the simple past: the past perfect shows which action happened first.
  - I ate (simple past) after I had cooked (past perfect).
