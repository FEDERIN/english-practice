# Past Perfect Continuous

## When to use it

1. **Actions that were in progress before another action in the past**.
   - I had been working all morning when she called.
   - They had been waiting for an hour before the train arrived.

2. **To emphasize the duration of a past action before another past event**.
   - He had been studying for 3 hours before the exam started.

## Form

- **Affirmative**: had + been + verb-ing.
  - I/you/he/she/it/we/they + had + been + V-ing.

- **Negative**: hadn't + been + verb-ing.
  - I hadn't been sleeping well.

- **Question**: Had + subject + been + verb-ing?
  - Had you been waiting long?

## Common time markers

for, since, before, when, by the time, all day/morning.

## Examples

- I had been working all morning.
- They had been waiting for 2 hours.
- They had been traveling for 6 hours.
- They had been practicing for the concert.
- You had been walking for a long time.

## Watch out

- Stative verbs (know, like, want, need, believe) are not usually used in the continuous form.
