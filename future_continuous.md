# Future Continuous

## When to use it

1. **Actions in progress at a specific time in the future**.
   - At 8 PM, I will be watching TV.
   - They will be working tomorrow.

2. **Planned or expected actions in the future**.
   - She will be traveling next week.

3. **Polite questions about someone's plans**.
   - Will you be using the car tonight?

## Form

- **Affirmative**: will + be + verb-ing.
  - I/you/he/she/it/we/they + will + be + V-ing.

- **Negative**: won't + be + verb-ing.
  - I won't be working.

- **Question**: Will + subject + be + verb-ing?
  - Will you be coming?

## Common time markers

tomorrow, next week, at 8 PM, this time tomorrow, in the future.

## Examples

- They will be working tomorrow.
- I will be studying tomorrow in the morning.
- They will be playing basketball tomorrow morning.
- We will be swimming in the pool.

## Watch out

- Do not confuse with the simple future: the future continuous emphasizes the action in progress at a future moment.
