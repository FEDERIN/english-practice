# Past Continuous

## When to use it

1. **Actions in progress at a specific time in the past**.
   - At 8 PM, I was watching TV.
   - They were playing soccer.

2. **Background actions interrupted by a shorter action**.
   - I was reading when the phone rang.
   - She was cooking while he was setting the table.

3. **Two simultaneous actions in the past**.
   - While I was studying, my brother was playing.

## Form

- **Affirmative**: was/were + verb-ing.
  - I/he/she/it + was + V-ing.
  - You/we/they + were + V-ing.

- **Negative**: was/were + not + verb-ing.
  - I wasn't working.
  - They weren't playing.

- **Question**: Was/Were + subject + verb-ing?
  - Were you sleeping?
  - Was she reading?

## Common time markers

while, when, as, at that moment, at 8 PM, all day yesterday.

## Examples

- They were playing soccer.
- They were buying a house.
- I was cleaning my bedroom.
- They were building a house.
- They were closing the store early.
- We were listening to the radio.

## Watch out

- Do not confuse with the simple past: the past continuous emphasizes the duration or background, while the simple past states a completed fact.
